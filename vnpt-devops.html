VNPT tip: read the question and all options; eliminate unreasonable answers; pick the most accurate and logical option — not the first familiar buzzword.

Watch absolute words (always/never), options that skip evidence (logs/metrics), and options that break security (secrets in Git, skip ATTT).

Cẩn thận từ tuyệt đối (always/never), đáp án bỏ qua bằng chứng (log/metric), và đáp án phá bảo mật (secret trong Git, bỏ qua ATTT).

Exam trap: Two options can be partly true — choose the one that matches the scenario constraints.
